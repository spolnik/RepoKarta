Deep Wiki turns a repository’s indexed evidence into a persisted documentation
set. Generation is a controlled artifact workflow: plan first, generate pages,
retain citations and provenance, surface progress and failure, then refresh
when the indexed revision changes.

## Safe operating pattern

<Steps>
1. Wait for the repository’s Search and map evidence to be ready enough for the
   intended documentation depth.
2. Select a provider and review the plan before committing generation budget.
3. Watch progress and coverage warnings; a completed model call is not the same
   as complete repository evidence.
4. Open citations from architectural claims and verify the pinned lines.
5. Regenerate after a meaningful indexed revision change; do not hide a stale
   Wiki behind the newest working tree.
6. Export only through the permission-checked artifact endpoint.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Generated is not authoritative by itself">
	A Wiki page is a navigable synthesis. Its authority comes from the cited
	RepoKarta evidence, recorded revision, and visible coverage—not fluent prose.
</Aside>
