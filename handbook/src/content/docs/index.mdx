RepoKarta keeps its answers attached to the exact repository, revision, path,
and line that produced them. This handbook teaches the product through three
real systems: RepoKarta itself, Spring Petclinic Microservices, and Bank of
Anthos.

## Three ways to read

**First hour** follows a practical path from launch to the first useful search.
**Feature chapters** explain each workflow in depth. **Configuration pages**
collect exact launch profiles, defaults, and operational trade-offs.

Every visual is captured from a local RepoKarta process against a recorded Git
commit. No product screen in this handbook is a mock-up.
